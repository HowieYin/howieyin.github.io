Inside a close reading

From claims to understanding

  1. The source. Paper title, authors, publication and version, original link, code, and reading date.
  2. The mechanism. Problem, prior approaches, core idea, equations, architecture, and original figures.
  3. The evidence. Experimental setup, baselines, results, ablations, and limitations.
  4. The takeaway. Personal insights, engineering relevance, and open questions, separate from the paper's claims.
